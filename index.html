About

Engineering with a review step

I work at the seam between software engineering and data — the place where an app needs a pipeline behind it, or a pipeline needs a real interface in front of it.

Recently that has meant putting large language models into production workflows: LLMs draft, score, and summarize, but a person approves the output before it ships. I think that review gate is the difference between a demo and a system people trust.
